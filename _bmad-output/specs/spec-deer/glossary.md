# Glossary — Deer

- **Deer** — product/brand name for this slice (confirmed by PO over "Farmaconcorso," an alternate name found in one design source).
- **Mnemotecnica (mnemonic)** — mnemonic association (image, wordplay) tied to one official question, shown after the visitor answers.
- **Quiz di assaggio (taste quiz)** — the 10-question, no-login flow this slice builds. Distinct from the future "simulazione" (100-question timed exam simulation), out of scope here.
- **Domanda ufficiale (official question)** — one of the 3000 official public-exam questions for pharmacy licensing. This slice shows 10, as placeholder content.
- **Sede farmaceutica** — the pharmacy license assigned via the public exam Deer prepares candidates for.
- **Schermata finale (final screen)** — screen shown after question 10: score, mnemonic recap, feedback box, optional email field.
- **Box feedback (feedback box)** — 5-level star rating component on the final screen.
- **Opt-in email** — optional email field on the final screen for launch updates. Not an account.
- **PO (Product Owner)** — owner of code, data, and all infra accounts (Supabase, Vercel, analytics, domain).
